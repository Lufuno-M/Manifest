# Manifest

*The archive of attention.*

Manifest is a catalog of recognized objects.

Objects are presented as artifacts rather than listings — given room to be looked at, understood, and remembered before anything else. Fashion, accessories, fragrances, and cultural objects exist together in a single drifting field where recognition matters as much as ownership.

The environment responds to attention. Color, atmosphere, and mood shift through dwell time, movement, and presence, turning browsing into part of the experience.

## Philosophy

* Recognition before possession
* Objects, not SKUs
* Honest documentation
* One continuous space
* Attention shapes the environment

## Current State

A single-file HTML/CSS/JavaScript project with no dependencies.

### The Field

A drifting catalog of recognized objects, searchable by name, maker, or category.

### The Record

Each object opens into a dedicated record containing imagery, context, sizes, and pricing.

### The Bag

A working checkout prototype with Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay payment-sheet simulations.

### The Wall

A continuous book of evidence positioned beyond the catalog: scans, sightings, quotes, and traces of what made these objects matter. It lives inside the same scroll as the field, with no separate page. The room is always the blend of the images it sits between, so every plate belongs to the room. Some plates carry a story. The paper sound has its own switch, separate from the ambience.

### Atmosphere

A dynamic environmental system driven by color clouds, dwell time, scroll behavior, ambient transitions, and optional audio.
